## Durable Lessons

- `torch.load(..., map_location="cuda")` moves serialized CUDA RNG byte tensors to CUDA; move them back to CPU before `set_rng_state_all`.
- Skip eager tensor-to-Python validation branches while Dynamo is compiling to avoid graph breaks; retain finite-loss checks in the trainer.
- Keep Windows and WSL environments separate (`.venv` and `.venv-wsl`) and never install a Linux NVIDIA driver inside WSL.
